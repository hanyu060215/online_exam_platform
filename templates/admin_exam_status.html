Individual Student Timing

Each student has their own independent exam timer. Giving one student more time does NOT prevent other students from logging in or taking their exam. Students can start their exams at different times, and each will have their own countdown based on their individual duration setting.

How to Give Students More Time:
  1. Go to Manage Durations
  2. Set the desired duration for the specific student
  3. Click "Save Changes"
  4. The change will apply to that student's exam timer only
  5. Other students are completely unaffected
