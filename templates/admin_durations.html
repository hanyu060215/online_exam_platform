How Individual Student Timing Works
  • Each student has their own independent exam timer - giving one student more time does NOT affect other students
  • Timer starts when student logs in - students can log in at different times
  • Custom durations apply per student - you can give different students different amounts of time
  • No global exam schedule - there are no restrictions on when students can start their exams
