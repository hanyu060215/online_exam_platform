How Global Timing Works
  • All students finish at the same time - regardless of individual durations
  • Students with extra time start earlier - they get more time but end simultaneously
  • Login restrictions apply - students can only log in during their allowed window
  • Example: If exam is 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM (120 min) and a student has 180 min, they can start at 1:00 PM
